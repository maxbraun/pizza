# Sources

The research and resources behind Forno's fermentation, flour, and baking
heuristics. This list mirrors the `SOURCES` data in
[`doughEngine.js`](./doughEngine.js) and the "Sources" panel in the app
itself — if you update one, update the other.

Links open the study or resource. Peer-reviewed work is paraphrased in this
tool's heuristics, not implemented from the original methods.
